# Grevix_ClariBite
Hello, my name is Tanishka Rana, and I’m currently pursuing a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) and here is my idea about the food scanner app , it's not just an App it's a movement for food awareness. 

🌟 My Idea – ClariBite (Grevix_ClariBite for submission)
ClariBite is an AI-powered food transparency app that helps families understand what they eat by decoding complex food labels into simple, everyday language.

🚩 Problem
Food labels are filled with codes and jargon that parents and children cannot easily understand. This creates confusion and unhealthy choices.

💡 Solution
ClariBite makes food clarity accessible and engaging:

📲 Scan & Decode → Instantly break down nutrition and additives.

🤖 ClariBot AI Assistant → Explains food terms in plain language.

📰 Verified Alerts → Pulls safety updates from FSSAI & WHO.

🎥 Kids’ Mode → Fun AI‑generated videos with the Sugar Monster mascot.

👩‍⚕️ Dietician Connect → Families can consult certified nutritionists.

💡 Alternatives → Suggests healthier, budget‑friendly swaps.

🌍 Impact
Promote healthier eating habits.

Build food literacy among children.

Encourage responsible consumption.

📈 Vision
Pilot in Uttarakhand schools by 2026.

Reach 1 crore families across India by 2030.

Become a global food‑clarity standard by 2035.


ClariBite is an AI-powered food transparency app that decodes labels, gives verified data, offers kids’ videos, and connects families with dieticians for healthier, smarter choices.
